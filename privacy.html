Privacy

Short and plain. Last updated October 2026.

What stays on your device

Your progress, XP, streak, answers, and the text of your resume or job description are stored in your own browser (local storage). Resume files are read inside your browser. Scoring, questions and the voice guide run on your device. Voice-to-text uses your browser's speech service (Chrome sends audio to Google's speech recognition while you speak).

Your resume is never uploaded

Resume and job description files are read inside your browser and kept only on your device. Padimachi does not receive, store or back up your resume.

Please do not upload

Government ID numbers, bank details, passwords or anyone else's private information. Remove your Aadhaar or PAN number from a resume before uploading it.

Delete your data

In the app, open Me and tap Reset everything to remove your data from this browser. If you have a question, contact us: contact details will be added soon. Tell us roughly when you uploaded it and your field so we can find it, because files are not stored under names.

Visit counting

When the owner has switched it on, Padimachi counts visits. It sends a random number stored in your browser, the name of the page you open (for example "learn") and the kind of action (for example "quiz"), plus whether you are on a phone or computer. It never sends your resume, name, answers or any personal detail. Browsers with Do Not Track on are not counted. Clearing site data gives you a new random number.

Signing in with Google (optional)

You can use the free areas of Padimachi as a guest without signing in. When the owner has switched sign-in on, you can choose Continue with Google to unlock more areas. Google confirms who you are. We never see your Google password.

What we keep: your name and email address, the date you signed in, and a count of which areas of the site you open, saved against a scrambled ID (not your email). This helps us know which areas are useful. Your resume, job description and answers are not sent to us. They stay in your browser.

Where it is kept: in a private Google Sheet owned by the site owner. The owner can read it. It is not sold or shared and is not used for advertising.

Delete it: open Profile while signed in and tap Delete my account. Your name, email and usage counts are removed. You can also write to padimachiadmin@gmail.com. Your saved progress on your own phone stays there until you clear it.

Cookies and tracking

Padimachi does not use advertising trackers.
