Teaching: interview questions and learning guide

Classroom, lesson planning and assessment for school teachers

Practise Teaching on Padimachi

What you will learn

Classroom management

A calm class is not silence. It is children knowing what happens next.

Classroom management is how a teacher keeps a class safe, focused and respectful. Start with a few clear rules, simple routines for entering, group work and finishing, and consistent kind responses. Notice good behaviour and say it. Move around the room, use a calm voice and look at the whole class. When a child misbehaves, correct privately, find the cause (boredom, hunger, trouble at home) and follow school policy. Never use physical punishment or shame.

Interview tip: Prepare two real examples: how you set rules in week one and how you handled one hard student kindly.

Lesson planning and teaching methods

A good lesson is a short journey: start with a hook, walk one clear path, check that everyone arrived.

A lesson plan states what students should learn (objectives), how you will teach it (hook, explanation, activity, practice) and how you will check it (questions, exit ticket). Pick objectives that use action words like explain, compare or solve. Bloom's taxonomy moves from remember and understand to apply, analyse and create. Mix methods: explanation, discussion, demonstration, group work and projects. Use simple teaching aids and real examples from daily life. NEP 2020 encourages activity based and competency based learning.

Interview tip: Practise a 5 minute demo lesson with one objective, one activity and one check for understanding.

Assessment and student support

Marks are one photo of a child. Assessment is the whole album.

Formative assessment happens during learning (questions, quizzes, observation) to help students improve. Summative assessment comes at the end (term exams) to record results. A rubric lists what good work looks like at each level so marking is fair and students know the goal. Give feedback that is specific and kind: one thing done well, one thing to improve, one next step. Support slow learners with extra practice, peer help and small steps. Inclusive education means every child, including those with special needs, can take part. Talk with parents with respect and facts.

Interview tip: Prepare how you would explain a low mark to a parent without blaming the child.

Digital teaching and online classes

Technology is a bridge, not the teacher. Use it where it makes learning clearer.

Digital tools help teachers share notes, give quizzes and reach students at home. Google Classroom, Zoom or Meet, and free resources such as DIKSHA and NCERT books support lessons. Keep slides simple with large text and one idea each. In online classes start on time, check audio, ask questions often, use polls and keep cameras and recordings respectful and safe. Blended learning mixes class and online work. Protect children's privacy and never share their details publicly.

Interview tip: Prepare how you keep students active in an online class and what you do when their internet fails.

Interview questions and sample answers

What is your teaching philosophy?

Every child can learn when given clear goals, kind support and practice. I plan for different learners and keep checking understanding.

How do you handle a student who does not do homework?

Find the reason first, talk privately, offer support and agree on small steps, then involve parents if needed.

How do you make a boring topic interesting?

Start with a real life example or question, use activities and visuals, and connect it to student interests.

How do you teach a mixed ability class?

Use tiered tasks, group work, peer help and checks so weaker students get support and stronger ones get challenge.

What is the difference between a lesson plan and a scheme of work?

A lesson plan covers one class. A scheme of work covers a unit or term across many lessons.

How do you deal with a bully in class?

Stop it at once, listen to both sides, follow school policy, support the victim and work with the bully and parents on behaviour.

How do you include a child with special needs?

Know their needs, adjust tasks and seating, work with specialists and parents, and treat the child with respect.

What is CCE?

Continuous and Comprehensive Evaluation looks at learning through the year, not only one final exam.

What is NEP 2020 in simple words?

India's National Education Policy that aims for flexible, skill based and activity based learning with more focus on foundations.

How do you communicate with parents?

Regularly, politely and with facts: strengths first, then concerns and a plan to help the child.

How do you manage your time in class?

Plan each segment, keep a visible timer, prepare materials in advance and keep a short buffer.

How do you motivate students who are afraid of a subject?

Start with small wins, link to daily life, praise effort and allow mistakes.

What is CTET and who needs it?

The Central Teacher Eligibility Test is a qualification needed for teaching in many central government schools. Check the official CBSE site for current rules.

How do you handle a heavy workload with marking?

Use rubrics, mark in batches, give group feedback and plan a weekly schedule.

Senior and lead interview questions

How do you lead a department and improve results?

Analyse results by topic, plan support, observe lessons, share good practice and track progress.

How do you mentor new teachers?

Give a buddy, observe and give feedback, share resources and set small goals.

How do you apply NEP 2020 ideas in your school?

Use activity based and competency based learning, flexible assessment and focus on foundations.

How do you handle a conflict with a parent or colleague?

Listen, stay calm, use facts, involve a senior if needed and agree next steps.

How do you plan a school year or term curriculum?

Map outcomes, plan units, assessments and revision, and review with the team.

How do you use data to support weak students?

Track assessment results, find gaps, group students, give targeted help and recheck.

How do you manage a large class effectively?

Use routines, group tasks, clear roles, quick checks and peer learning.
